Questions

1. Can you use Bootstrap on your final project?

Yes! I can use it on my final project, and I probably will since it makes the page look clean and work on phones without a ton of extra work.

2. Which components did you add to your page, and which of them needed Bootstrap's JavaScript to work?

I added a navbar, an alert, a card with a button, a modal, and an accordion. The navbar, modal, and accordion all needed Bootstrap's JavaScript. The navbar needs it for the menu button on phone size, the modal needs it to open and close, and the accordion needs it to expand and collapse. The alert and the card didn't need it, just the CSS.
