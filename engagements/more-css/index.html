Questions

What is the difference between block tags and inline tags?

Block tags start on a new line and stretch across the full width of the page (like p, div, h1). Inline tags sit inside a line of text and are only as wide as their content (like strong, em, a, span).

What is the margin, border, and padding?

Padding is the space between the content and the border. The border is the line around the padding. The margin is the empty space outside the border that separates the box from other boxes.

What are units of measurement you can use in CSS?

px, em, rem, %, vw, vh, pt, cm, and more. Pixels (px) are fixed. em and rem scale with font size, and %, vw, and vh scale with the size of the page or window.

When would you use a figure tag?

You use a figure tag when you want an image (or diagram, chart, code) to have its own block with a caption, separate from the surrounding text.
