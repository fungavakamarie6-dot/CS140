Questions

1. What are different ways to style your form?

You can use CSS to change colors, fonts, borders, and spacing. You can also use flexbox to line up labels and inputs, and style the submit button.

2. Why does the professor like to use flex?

Flex lines up labels and inputs neatly in rows and columns, and it adjusts to different screen sizes.

3. What makes for a good mobile form?

One column, big buttons and inputs, clear labels, and the right input types so the right keyboard shows up.
