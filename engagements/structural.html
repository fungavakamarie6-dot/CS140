What goes in the header?

usually the title or name of the person creating the sites and the purpose of the sites.

What goes in the Nav? Can the Nav go in the header? Does it have to go in the header? Does the Nav contain links to another website?

Nav holds thelinks people click to move around your site, for example, the Home, About, Contact, etc. It can go inside the header, but it doesn't have to, it can also stand on its own outside the header.

What goes in the main?

Main holds the actual content of your page, the stuff that is unique to the page. It can only be use one main per page

What goes in the Footer?

The footer is the bottom section, it usually a copyright line, contact info, or extra small links, kind of like the fine print at the end of a page.

HTML Validation

This refers to checking your HTML code for errors using a tool such as W3C Validator, to make sure your tags are written correctly and nothing is broken or missing.
