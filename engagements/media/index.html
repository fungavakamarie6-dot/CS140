Questions

1. What are the positives and negatives of hosting your videos on YouTube?

On the plus side, it's free, keeps my own site files smaller and faster to load, and works on pretty much any device. The downsides are less control over the player (like ads or related videos popping up), the risk of the video getting deleted or set to private, and needing an internet connection to play it.

2. What is the most useful thing you can do with a transform?

Moving, scaling, or rotating something without shifting the layout around it. For example, making a card slightly bigger on hover without knocking nearby items out of alignment.

3. What is the difference between a transform and a transition?

A transform changes the shape, position, or angle of an element (like moving or scaling it), while a transition controls how smoothly that change happens over time instead of jumping all at once.
