Title and topic

Tongan Food and Feasts

A guide to traditional Tongan dishes and the occasions where they are served, from everyday meals to weddings, birthdays, and church celebrations.

2

Why I chose this topic

I am from Tonga, and food is at the center of how my family celebrates. Every gathering, church event, and birthday is built around a table full of dishes that people spend days preparing together.

I chose this topic to share that part of my culture with people who have never experienced it, and to build a site that is useful as well as good looking. It also lets me practice everything we learned this semester: structured pages, styled layouts, tables, forms, and media.

Collecting content and references

I will take my own photos of food at family and church gatherings whenever I can. Descriptions will come from my family's knowledge, and I will check facts against reliable sources. Any image I did not take myself will be used only with permission or from a source that allows reuse, and it will be credited on the About page. The final site will list at least five references. These are two I plan to use:

  • Tonga Tourism Authority Official tourism website, for background on Tongan culture, food, and events.
  • Wikipedia: Tongan cuisine Overview of traditional dishes and cooking methods, cross-checked against a second source before I use it.
