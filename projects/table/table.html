About this table

Tonga is a group of islands in the South Pacific. It is the only Pacific island nation that was never colonized.

In 1845 Taufa'ahau united the islands and became King George Tupou I. He gave Tonga its first constitution in 1875.

Queen Salote ruled for 47 years and is still remembered as a very loved leader.

Years Ruled is just the year the reign ended minus the year it started.
